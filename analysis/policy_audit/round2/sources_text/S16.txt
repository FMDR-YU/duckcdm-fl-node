How it works

A federated data network to improve care throughout the world

We have partnered with leading healthcare organizations across the globe to create a data network that represents far more diverse patient populations than what exists within Mayo Clinic alone. The network provides federated access to rich, deep multimodal data that makes discovery, diagnosis, and treatment much richer and more generalizable.

Our current data network members include:

Mercy
Seoul National University Hospital
Sheba Medical Center
University Health Network (UHN)
Einstein Hospital Israelita
Aga Khan University
